Covetors Email Builder

Paste a plain email. It comes out in the Covetors template, ready to copy into Gmail.

Formatting
  • Leave a blank line between paragraphs. Lines with no blank line between them stay together.
  • A block of Label: details lines, or one that starts with a **bold title**, becomes a details card. Good for meeting info.
  • Start a line with > to put it in the highlighted blue panel.
  • Wrap words in **double stars** to make them bold.
  • Web addresses and email addresses become links automatically.
  • Your sign-off (Best regards, Kind regards, Thanks…) and the name under it become the signature.
